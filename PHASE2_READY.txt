📈 KEY METRICS EXPLAINED:

PAR 30 (Portfolio at Risk 30 days)
- % of outstanding balance overdue 30+ days
- Target: < 5% (Excellent)
- 5-10%: Good, > 10%: Needs attention

PAR 90 (Non-Performing Loans)
- % of outstanding balance overdue 90+ days
- Used for BoG NPL ratio reporting
- Target: < 3%

Collection Rate
- (Total Collected / Total Disbursed) × 100
- Target: > 95%

BoG Loan Classification:
- Current (0-30 days): 0% provision
- Substandard (31-90 days): 10% provision
- Doubtful (91-180 days): 50% provision
- Loss (180+ days): 100% provision
